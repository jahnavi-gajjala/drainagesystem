Marripalem Drainage Fixed

The Marripalem area, a bustling residential zone in the heart of the city, had long been troubled by severe drainage issues. Residents often voiced concerns about the unhygienic conditions, stagnant water, and the unbearable smell that lingered across the streets, especially during the monsoon. For years, overflowing drains had not only inconvenienced locals but also raised serious health concerns due to the breeding of mosquitoes and the increased risk of waterborne diseases. Complaints had been pouring into municipal offices for months, but until recently, no significant action had been taken. However, following a wave of complaints and awareness raised through social media and local campaigns, the Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation (GVMC) finally took note. A dedicated sanitation and civil engineering team was dispatched to assess the ground situation. Upon investigation, they discovered multiple blockages in underground drainage lines caused by years of accumulated debris, construction waste, and improperly disposed garbage. In response, the municipal team quickly mobilized desilting machines and deployed sanitation workers to clean and flush out the drains.
